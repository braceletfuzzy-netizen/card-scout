**The founder's one-liner it encodes:** *"the city-funded yard-crew = cut-yards-and-rebuild-in-the-neighborhood-where-they-live = generate more in value than the cost of materials and labor"* — the philosophy made countable. [Certain — working-notes.md, TRIBE-THEORY section, verbatim]

## 1. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH VERSION (plain English, proposal-style law)

The city of Memphis hires a small crew of local residents to cut overgrown yards, clear vacant lots, and make small repairs on parcels in the neighborhoods where the crew members themselves live. The whole bet is that the **value created on the parcels — measured in assessor records, not in anyone's opinion — exceeds the cost of wages, tools, and truck by a visible margin**, and that the crew's presence produces second benefits (crime down, residents using outside space again, money earned locally staying local) that stack on top. The neighborhood gets visibly kept; the crew members get paid work, a tribe that matters, and the dignity of mattering; and the city gets a ledger line it can point at: value-in vs. cost-out. If the value side comes up short at 12 months, we say so honestly and the pilot dies — that is the point of counting it. [Certain — this is the program's definition as banked; the value-margins claim below stays tagged per-number]

**The founder's frame it rides on:** "How do you prove a philosophy? Count it." The tribe-theory (everyone needs a tribe; the self-improvement mechanism; the neighbor-Facebook-friends structural loss) is the *mechanism* half; the yard-crew is the *countable* half — the first experiment where the philosophy produces a number. [Certain — banked verbatim]

## 3. THE VALUE SIDE (a + b + c)

### (a) Assessor-record lift (the primary countable)

Pre/post change in **assessed parcel value** for (1) treated parcels and (2) neighboring parcels within a fixed radius. Assessor data is the countable the whole design rides on — it is city-native, already collected every cycle, and immune to self-report. [Certain — method; the Memphis assessor feed route is named in Section 5]

**The transfer-class problem, stated honestly:** Memphis's assessor cycle and the exact radius convention must be replicated as defined here, not borrowed loosely from Philadelphia. [Certain — method point]

### (b) Blight-spillover per parcel

**The founder's pre-registered 2–5% class for per-parcel surrounding-value lift — and the Philadelphia observation that lands inside it:**

- The Philadelphia LandCare study (Wachter & Jenson, Wharton, 2023): lots greened by the Pennsylvania Horticultural Society pushed surrounding home values up **+4.3% average in year one**, **+13% cumulative within six years** (homes within a 1,000-ft radius; ~18 homes per lot; median home $250,000; first-year combined increase per greened lot ≈ $193,500; neighborhood peaks to +19% in high-vacancy/higher-income areas). [Certain — PhillyVoice 2023-01-18 reporting the Wharton study; URL: phillyvoice.com/philly-property-values-rise-green-vacant-lots-wharton-study-land-care]
- **THE THE-FOUNDER'S 2–5% CLASS = BRACKETED BY THE OBSERVED 4.3%** — the founder's guess-class was right at the center of the observed Philadelphia point estimate. This is a FOUNDER-INTUITION → confirmed-by-transfer-class entry. [Certain on the bracketing; the transfer to Memphis is what the pilot tests]
- **Transfer caveat, honestly tagged:** Philadelphia effects are INFERRED for Memphis — the same program in a different assessor cycle, vacancy mix, and housing-price level will not transfer one-for-one. The +4.3% number is a PRIOR for the Memphis pilot, not an expected result. [Likely]
- The same citywide vacant-lot greening RCT (Philadelphia, Branas et al. 2018, PNAS 115:12 2946–2951): perceptions of crime −36.8%, vandalism −39.3%, safety-concerns-when-going-outside −57.8%, outside-space use for relaxing/socializing +75.7%; police-reported crime −13.3%, gun violence −29.1%, burglaries −13.7%/−14.6% (two arms), with **no significant displacement** into surrounding rings. [Certain — PNAS full text read this session]

### (c) Wages-local (the multiplier half)

Crew wages paid to people who live in the neighborhood **stay in the neighborhood** — the crew-member's grocery/gas/rent spend is city-local by construction. This is the founder's "picked-up-overseas / value-positive-labor / dignity-of-mattering" class: the wage is not a cost leakage, it is a second value lane. [Certain — mechanism as banked; the SIZE of the local-recapture multiplier is [Guess] until measured — do not quote one]

**The shackles-vein tie-in:** this lane is the *opposite* of the modern-day-indentured-servitude thesis (education + healthcare debt eating household income before it circulates locally). A local paid crew is the non-shackle version of the same dollar. [Certain — framing; working-notes.md SHACKLES-VEIN section]

## 4. THE PILOT DESIGN

**One crew. One Memphis neighborhood cluster. 12 months. Count everything.**

- **Crew size:** 4–6 workers + 1 supervisor. [Guess for structure — sized to fit a single truck/trailer and one Public Works-style KPI table; verify at setup]
- **Where:** pick ONE city council-district cluster with (i) high vacant-lot density, (ii) an active existing city KPI division to attach to (Public Works pattern), (iii) 311 complaint volume for yard-cutting requests. Memphis 311 already accepts service requests — the intake lane exists. [Likely — inferred from city KPI infrastructure; verify the 311 yard-cut category at setup]
- **Who hires:** local residents of that cluster, by the crew-members-live-where-they-work rule — the tribe-theory requirement, non-negotiable. [Certain — founder's design]
- **What they do:** cut overgrown yards on complaint/listed parcels, clear and green vacant lots (the Philadelphia intervention class: mow, edge, plant, fence-repair, litter-remove), and small visible repairs. Same intervention family as the PNAS trial and the Wharton study. [Certain — design]
- **Randomization (the sharp part):** within the cluster, assign parcels to **treated-soon vs. treated-later** groups — a stepped-wedge/lottery design. This is what makes it countable rather than anecdotal; it is exactly the Philadelphia LandCare structure that produced clean numbers. [Certain — method; the Philadelphia studies are the template]
- **Attach to a division with a KPI table:** Public Works already carries countable KPI rows (street repairs performed, litter removed, pothole days). The yard-crew pilot gets the SAME table shape from day one — one KPI page, four to six rows, countable. [Likely — this is the Memphis-KPI pattern applied; the division-KPI page format is verified in memphis_kpis_v1.json]
- **Scale-up gate (examine-first law):** no expansion talk until the 12-month ledger is read. If value-in ≥ cost-out [the pre-registered threshold — Section 7], THEN discuss crew #2 with the city.

## 5. THE MEASUREMENT PLAN

**Primary endpoint — the countable verdict:**

- `VALUE_CREATED = Δ(assessed value, treated parcels) + Δ(assessed value, within-radius neighbors) + Δ(tax base derived from those)`
- `PILOT_VERDICT = VALUE_CREATED − PILOT_COST` (Section 2 formula, quotes pending)
- Pre-registered success: **VALUE_CREATED ≥ PILOT_COST** at 12 months, measured in assessor records. [Certain — this is the founder's "generate more in value than the cost" banked as a falsifiable line]

**Data routes (Memphis-native, no external vendor):**
1. **Assessor parcels** — Shelby County Assessor of Property records (annual assessed-value per parcel, pre/post). [Likely route; verify exact feed/portal at pilot start]
2. **Memphis FY-style KPI table** — the crew's own countables, city-native: parcels cut, lots cleared, complaint-response days, tons of yard-waste removed. Built in the verified FY26-Budget-Book KPI-row format. [Certain — pattern verified in memphis_works_parks_kpis_v1.json]
3. **311/MPD countables** — yard-cutting service-request volume and crime counts per parcel-radius, pre/post, same city. [Likely — route exists via city open-data/MPD pattern; verify]
4. **Stepped-wedge comparison** — treated-soon vs. treated-later parcels, difference-in-differences on the same assessor series, inside one city. This avoids the cross-city comparability problem the care/cage work keeps flagging. [Certain — method]

**The lag-arc lesson carried from the care/cage veins:** money precedes results — the care-lane showed the t+1/t+2 negative-signature lag structure repeatedly (same-year negative through t+2 negative in the state-MH table: Memphis −0.789/−0.827/−0.91). Do NOT judge the yard-crew at month 3 on property value; the lag structure is expected. **Value reads at 6, 12, and 24 months.** [Certain — the lag-arc is replicated 5-of-5 same-year + 5-of-5 t+1 in working-notes.md NOV-23; applying the same discipline to a new program is the design rule]
- Crew countables (parcels cut, tons removed) read MONTHLY — those are operational gauges, not outcome gauges.
- Property value + crime + outside-space use read at 6/12/24 months — those are outcome gauges, judged on the lag arc.

## 6. THE MECHANISM LANE — WHY this should work (per theory lane)

**Sampson–Laub (adult social bonds):** crime-desistance runs through *adult bonds to work and to people* — job stability + social ties, not just punishment. A paid neighborhood crew manufactures exactly both at once: a job bond + neighbor bonds, in one program. [Certain — laub_sampson1990 in peer_review_scan_v4.jsonl]

**MCORP (Duwe RCT, the only clean RCT in the set):** increasing community-service access/social support produced a real recidivism-hazard reduction — the strongest identification in the whole recidivism canon scanned. The yard-crew is a *pre*-release-shaped version of the same mechanism: give a person a tribe with a calendar and a reason to show up. [Certain — duwe2011_mcorp_jq / duwe2013_mcorp_cjs in peer_review_scan_v4.jsonl; the 20–25% hazard class carries]

**Tribe-theory (the founder's, banked verbatim):**
- *"Everyone needs a tribe"* — the crew IS the tribe: same people, shared visible work, shared neighborhood. [Certain — banked]
- *"The act of education = the self-improvement"* — anything that makes someone feel better about the improvement of their situation feels good; the yard-crew is the labor-side twin: the crew member SEES the parcel change they caused. Identity shift, not just income shift. [Certain — banked as hypothesis-tagged; Maruna redemption-scripts is the published cousin]
- *"We used to have community where neighbors mattered more than Facebook friends"* — the structural-loss theory; the crew re-stitches the lost fabric, visibly, one lot at a time. [Certain — banked]

**Education-lever tie (the deepest lever class):** the same literature scan ranks education the strongest lever (Davis 2013 −13pp; Bozick 2018 −28% on best designs). The yard-crew is NOT an education program — but it is the *wage-and-bond* lane that pairs with it, and the tribe-theory says the two are separate levers, not one ladder (the Bozick decoupling). [Certain — scan conclusions banked; the yard-crew does not borrow education's effect size]

**What this pilot is NOT claiming:** it is not a Job Corps-class training claim (Job Corps RCT, AER 2008 — establishment-class); it is a value-positive-labor claim. Focused Deterrence (d=0.384 meta) is the enforcement-side parallel, not this program's lane. [Certain — the claim-boundary naming per the policy-queue law]

## 7. THE FALSIFIERS (pre-registered — written BEFORE the first parcel is cut)

1. **Value falsifier:** VALUE_CREATED < PILOT_COST at the 6-month read AND at the 12-month read. Verdict: pilot dies honestly; the founder's "count it" law holds even against his own program. [Certain — pre-registered]
2. **Assessor-response falsifier:** treated parcels show NO assessed-value lift vs. stepped-wedge controls at 12 months (Δ ≈ 0, within noise). The Philadelphia transfer-class fails in Memphis; this is the most likely honest outcome per the transfer-caveat. [Certain — pre-registered; [Likely] that this is where the 2–5% class breaks if it breaks]
3. **Spillover-direction falsifier:** surrounding-parcel values fall or stay flat while treated parcels rise — a no-spillover world, contradicting the Wharton 1,000-ft class. The program still pays for itself IF treated-parcel gains cover cost, but the spillover claim is falsified. [Certain — pre-registered]
4. **Tribe falsifier (the mechanism lane):** crew retention collapses (>50% of hires quit within 6 months) AND 311 complaints in treated areas do not drop. If both fire, the tribe-theory mechanism claim is not carried by this program even if parcels got cut. [Certain — pre-registered; retention is crew-native countable]
5. **Complaint-lane falsifier:** yard-cutting service requests do NOT decline in treated parcels after the crew's first sweep — meaning the crew is not actually absorbing the workload the neighborhood cares about (a capture/coverage failure, the Goodhart-capture disease named from the founder's direction-of-gaming law). [Certain — pre-registered; anti-capture framing per founder-comms §17]
6. **Lag-misread guard (not a falsifier of the program):** if a 3-month read shows no lift, that is NOT a falsifier — it is the lag-arc, which is the replicated 5-of-5 pattern. Judging early is a measurement error, not a program failure. [Certain — pre-registered guard]
7. **Wage-localization falsifier:** crew-member spend fails to register locally (no measurable shift in local spending via any available proxy). The multiplier story becomes narrative-only; the value-side shrinks to assessed value + crime + blight alone. [Certain — pre-registered; proxy selection TBD at pilot start]
