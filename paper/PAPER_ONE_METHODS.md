## §1 THE CLAIMS REGISTER

Eight numbered claims. Each states its supporting mathematics (§2 defines every estimator), the banked numbers whose derivation it rests on, and — where a counter-literature exists — the claim's honest scope statement (the dimensions on which the claim stands in its own domain).

**CLAIM-1 — The two-shackles frame.** Housing and food security dominate the input side of the city-level violence relationship, and the two behave differently by geography of comparison. Housing is the dominant shelter correlate: pooled Spearman rank correlations between murder rate and median home value −0.819 and gross rent −0.856. Food security shows a within/between class flip: within-city deviation share (SNAP receipt) correlates +0.495 with the murder rate, while the between-city cross-section inverts at approximately −0.5. The interpretation the study carries: shelter strain tracks violence levels across cities, while food need chases violence within a city over time (need follows violence), so SNAP enters with opposite signs in within- versus between-city designs.
*Scope statement:* the education–health "two shackles" thesis (costs/access as modern indentured servitude with a macro spending-bleed) is the founding frame, but the fitted panel ranks shelter above both: in the joint regression the tier ordering fails as a total order (T2 care 0.627 > T1 housing 0.515 > T1 food 0.495 ≈ T4/5 education 0.528 > T4/5 choice-set 0.19), and education does not beat the base of the pyramid on this panel. The counter-read that education is the deepest causal lever per dollar (Jackson, Johnson & Persico 2015; Lochner & Moretti 2004) is accepted and carried in its own domain — long-horizon person-level earnings and crime returns — and differs here on grain and horizon: ours is city-year panel, within-city, 35-year crime arc; theirs is cohort earnings decades out. Both stand; they answer different questions.
*Counter-literature carried:* standard housing-crime and food-insecurity research attributes these correlations to selection of households into strained cities; our within-city design absorbs every time-invariant city attribute, and the class-flip itself (SNAP +0.495 within vs −0.5 between) is the empirical signature that selection and need-chasing are separable on our data.

**CLAIM-2 — The p90 ceiling gradient.** The wage structure, not the average wage, is what tracks city murder rates: Spearman rank correlations between the murder rate and metropolitan wage levels stratified at the 10th percentile −0.452, median −0.667, 90th percentile −0.857, and the p90/p10 ratio −0.714 (OEWS 2025 metropolitan snapshot, 10-metro T-head, n=8 rank-pairs). Cities with the weakest specialized high-wage band are the violence-heaviest. Memphis carries the panel's worst pairing: 90/10 wage ratio 3.48 (versus Boston 4.57, NYC 4.60, Houston 4.68) alongside the panel's second-worst crime index.
*Scope statement:* the snapshot is single-vintage (2025), so this is a between-city cross-section, never a within-city arc; the occupational-mix companion (occupation shares vs p90, r up to 0.98 for the computer-math band; healthcare share negatively r −0.60) is descriptive. Minimum-wage crime literature disagrees on levels while agreeing on floors: in concentrated (oligopsonistic) markets a 1% minimum-wage increase associates with employment +0.16% and larceny-theft −0.31% in our wage-floor lane, consistent with the wage floor cutting crime most where employers hold wage-setting power — a structure claim our ceiling gradient extends upward from the floor to the top of the distribution.

**CLAIM-3 — Care is protective holding the Maslow lanes.** In the joint within-city standardized FE regression (n=24, R² 0.695), the care-dollars coefficient is −0.18/−0.20/−0.51 across the three fitted frames (FE-3 t0 / arc t+1 / FE-2), direction-stable. The lag structure strengthens with horizon: within-city lagged care-vs-crime correlations grow from −0.21 at t+1 to −0.345 at t+2 in the deep panel, and the five-city within-state replication is direction-negative in all twenty lane-arcs (same-year −0.37 to −0.87; t+1 −0.82 to −0.98; t+2 −0.758 to −0.999), with lag-strength-growth in both replication states (NY: −0.844 → −0.867; MO: −0.822 → −0.758 held).
*Scope statement:* two live counter-reads are accepted as real and carried. (1) Reverse causation — officers (+0.243) and SAMHSA grant funding (+0.394) correlate POSITIVELY with crime in pooled cross-sections because money follows crisis. Our method accounts for exactly this: the within-city lagged design separates the proactive signature (money raised preceding crime falls, negative-strengthening) from the reactionary signature (bookings flip sign after year one: −0.227 same-year, +0.125/+0.100 at lags). (2) The care–crime correlation is weak within-city year-over-year (−0.105 deep-panel FE). We accept and report it: the care signal lives in the multi-year arc, not the single-year delta — which is why the instrument's headline is the t+1 arc class.

**CLAIM-4 — The instrument's blindfolds.** The fitted murder equation's next-year direction accuracy is 0.938 (n=16 city-year lag-1 pairs; beats the persistence-only baseline 0.531), and leave-one-city-out refits preserve it: LOCO per-city lag-1 direction Cincinnati 0.75 / Detroit 1.00 / Memphis 0.75 / St. Louis 0.75, arc-class mean 0.812. Composition stress (seeded drop-refits): the founder-carrying signs (care negative, SNAP positive, the FE-6 pair) never flip across 17 refits; the flips concentrate in non-headline columns. Power-curve: the between-city share (53–87%) absorbed by city fixed effects argues deepening the panel before widening it (kink near n≈150 city-years). Panel-churn audit: 0.832 carry (116 of 663 window city-years are publication-drop events; survivorship is publication-class, not outcome-selection).
*Scope statement:* the deepening t+2/t+3/t+4 direction accuracy decays (0.583/0.375/~0) — the instrument's honest horizon is one year ahead. Level-transfer across cities fails by design of the within-city frame; LOCO numbers are direction-class only. This claim's scope is therefore explicitly direction-plus-one-year.

**CLAIM-5 — Flow versus stock.** A one-year 10% care increase (the flow) carries a small next-year read (−0.16% geometric, n=31 noisy, t≈−0.22) that does NOT persist — the AR-1 coefficient on within-city care changes is −0.104 (n=58), i.e., flow changes revert. A sustained 10% care increase (the stock) prices −1.48% at t0 and −0.89% at t+1 with t≈−2.24 — the largest single-lever read in the machine. The stock-over-flow multiplier stands at 11.6× (FE-6 frame) and 9.5× (FE-3 frame).
*Scope statement:* the multiplier is a model-implied ratio of two card reads at their respective frames, not a causal experiment; the honest-null (non-persistence of flows) is printed beside it. The stock exemplar carried from the literature is the school-spending dose design (Jackson, Johnson & Persico 2015: +10% sustained 12 years → +0.31 years educational attainment, +7% adult wages, −3.2pp adult poverty) — accepted as the dose-shape our sustained-frame cards emulate, differing in that ours is city-budget grain over one-year increments.

**CLAIM-6 — The efficiency-residual administration instrument.** Define the delivery residual r(c,t) = actual murder rate − predicted rate (the fitted equation at city c, year t, all inputs held). Cities ranked by the cumulative residual ARC (first-half mean minus second-half mean): Cincinnati 1, Memphis 2 (the sharpest improver, +4.64 → −9.45 rate-points), St. Louis 3, Detroit 4. Residual stickiness is honest: pooled lag-1 residual correlation 0.459 (weak-positive), so administrations are scored on half-arcs, never single years. The instrument is a community-tier measure (outcome per delivered dollar); the two-tier ledger pairs it with an operational tier (delivered per budgeted = 1 − overhead rate, from annual financial report line extracts) and the multiplication identity of §2.8.
*Scope statement:* with 4–5 scored city-years per city this ranks direction, not guilt; mean residuals pin at ~0 by within-FE construction and cannot rank levels (the guard is printed with the rank). The Memphis boundary read (+15.75 swing after the January-2024 administration change) carries a federal-task-force guard at n=2 post-years — the administration effect cannot yet be separated from the federal surge; it resolves as post-years accrue. Counter-literature: the fiscal-determinants literature (rainy-day funds, pension rates, credit events) prices distress odds as outcomes of fiscal structure — we accept that literature and differ on direction: our residual is the OUTCOME-side instrument with fiscal structure as the operational-tier input, not a probability model of crisis.

**CLAIM-7 — The MATA failure anatomy.** Memphis' transit authority is the measured archetype of the joint failure class: funding-starved first ($82 per resident operations 2024 vs Cincinnati $514, St. Louis $1,041, Detroit $264, Richmond $340, Indianapolis $155 — computed from NTD actuals ÷ Census city population); zero agency-levied taxes every year while a peer's dedicated levy (Central Ohio Transit Authority, $161.4M/yr sales tax) alone exceeds 3× MATA's entire budget; a ten-year unrestricted deficit (−$90M 2014, −$91M 2019) concealed until the state comptroller forced its disclosure (May 2024); governance cascade (board replaced Oct-2024, five resignations Sept-2025, a two-person city trusteeship at $75,000/month, board restored Sept-2026, four CEOs in 30 months); service designed out (vehicle revenue-hours 261K→216K during recovery, 23→18 routes in Nov-2024 at 60-minute headways, ~26–30% of schedules late in late-2025). The zero-fare natural experiment (ridership +18% in two weeks; +23% ≈ 2.7M trips ≈ $4M rider savings by Nov-2026) establishes that demand exists and supply binds.
*Scope statement:* the Tennessee-state claim is deliberately reframed — "flat $6–9M/yr state grant, no dedicated stream of any kind, $0 self-levied taxes" is verified; a "worst in nation" ranking was NOT verifiable this cycle and is not claimed. The Houston counter-read (car-oriented, 26–28-minute commutes, 2.9–3.4% transit share at 3× per-resident funding) is accepted: dispersion across southern cities means geography is not the excuse, which strengthens rather than weakens the funding-first reading.

**CLAIM-8 — The two-tier ledger is not in print.** A 428-deduped-record literature prism (136 live probes, 53 queries, keyless Crossref/PubMed/EuropePMC, year-span 1980–2027; the highest-cited anchor: Charnes, Cooper & Rhodes 1981 data-envelopment-analysis corpus at 1,067 citations) finds both tiers separately represented and the seam empty: the DEA municipal-service corpus (~104 band records) measures cost-efficiency of delivery (the operational tier); the outcome-dose literature (Jackson/Lafortune class) measures outcome per input (the community tier); ZERO corpus records measure overhead-rate and outcome-yield as distinct quantities and combine them — the multiplication identity of §2.8 — or assemble the 2×2 administration scorecard (wrong programs vs leaky pipes vs both). DEA is accepted as the calibration reference class for our residual instrument (DEA-VRS/CRS, super-efficiency, Malmquist, Tobit second-stage, SFA robustness); the claim is novelty of the joint decomposition, not of either tier.
*Scope statement:* the zero-hit stands under 40 prism-band live probes and a keyless-fetch constraint; a non-keyless sweep (IMF/OECD macro frameworks) is a named honest gap and the moat claim is scoped to the searched corpus.

## §2 THE MATH MODULE

**2.1 — The fixed-effects specification.** For city *c* and year *t*, inputs x_i are standardized within city: z_i(c,t) = (x_i(c,t) − mean_x_i(c)) / sd_x_i(c). The outcome is expressed as deviation from the city's own frame mean in within-city standard-deviation units of the outcome. The fitted model is the within-city linear model

  y_c,t − mean_y(c) = Σ_i β_i · z_i(c,t) · sd_y(c) + ε_c,t,

with one city fixed effect per city absorbed by within-city demeaning. Prediction: pred(c, t+k) = mean_y(c) + Σ_i β_i · z_i(c,t) · sd_y(c), inputs taken at T only. Frames: FE-3 t0 (care, cost-burden, home value, SNAP, bachelors; n=24, R² 0.695), FE-3_arc t+1 (n=18, R² 0.748), FE-6 (care + unemployment; n=36, R² 0.263), FE-2 (care + cage + bookings; n=19, R² 0.423). t-statistics are reported as descriptive.

**2.2 — The walk-forward scoring rule.** A direction hit at horizon k is sign(pred(c,t+k) − mean_y(c)) == sign(actual(c,t+k) − mean_y(c)). The walk-forward score at horizon k is the pooled hit rate over all scored (c, t+k) pairs. Registration gate: pooled next-year (k=1) direction accuracy > 0.6 strictly. Magnitude scoring: Spearman ρ(pred, actual) within-city-demeaned (primary) and raw, with MAE and RMSE in rate-points. Partial-year outcome cells are excluded; pairs score only where both prediction inputs and banked outcomes exist.

**2.3 — The LOCO procedure.** Leave-one-city-out refitting: for each city j, refit the betas on the panel excluding all city-j rows, predict city j's next-year outcomes with the refit betas, and score direction accuracy per city. The LOCO gate read is the arc-class mean of per-city lag-1 direction accuracies (four cities in the anchor frame → 0.75/1.00/0.75/0.75, mean 0.812). LOCO refits use refitted betas; the backwards test freezes them — the two procedures are both reported and labeled.

**2.4 — The elasticity conversion.** Standardized beta → unit read: rate-points per 1% input change = β_i · sd_x_i / mean_x_i · 100 (per-percent-of-mean form), and % outcome per 1% input = β_i · sd_x_i / mean_x_i · 100 / mean_y. Delta (within-year change) form: regress Δmurder-rate on Δln(care) within city over contiguous-year pairs; pooled n=31 gives −1.55 rate-points per +100% care (per +1% = −0.0155 rate-points). Flow card: 10% one-year care change → −0.16% geometric outcome change. Stock card: sustained change read from the level machines (−1.48% t0, −0.89% t+1 per +10%). Multiplier = |stock read| / |flow read| at matched frames (11.6× FE-6; 9.5× FE-3). Persistence check: AR-1 on within-city Δln(care) = −0.104 (n=58).

**2.5 — The stock/flow decomposition.** Stock = capacity standing at day 0 (beds, staff, fleet — a dollar funds it only in order to HAVE it there). Flow = the annual budget line. The decomposition states: outcome response to an input change = flow response (in-year, decaying at AR-1 −0.104) if the change does not persist; stock response (level machines, durable) if sustained. Sustained-10%-for-Y-years reads compound the level machine over the horizon; compounding-correct window reads are tabulated beside persistence-decay reads so the two hypotheses are separable per city-year window.

**2.6 — The efficiency residual definition.** r(c,t) = y_actual(c,t) − ŷ(c,t), where ŷ is the FE-3 prediction with all of the city-year's inputs (care dollars, cage dollars, bookings, rent burden, home value, SNAP, schooling, unemployment) held at actuals. Rank statistic: arc_rank = mean(residuals, first half of the city's scored span) − mean(residuals, second half); negative arcs = improving delivery. Guards: mean-level ≈ 0 by within-FE construction (level ranking is invalid — the arc is the read); composition/need context carried on every row; small-n flags (n<8) on every rank.

**2.7 — The two-tier multiplication identity.** outcome per dollar-in = (outcome per delivered) × (delivered per budgeted). Community tier = the first factor (the residual instrument of 2.6, all outcome columns). Operational tier = the second factor (1 − overhead rate; annual financial report line extracts: reserve ratios, pension percentages, subsidy lines). A city fails either way: wrong programs (low outcome per delivered at good throughput) or leaky pipes (good outcome per delivered at a loss in throughput). The 2×2 administration scorecard crosses the two tiers.

**2.8 — Pooled correlation convention (format-lock).** Per-city correlations are pooled to a panel read via the Fisher-z mean; any city with |ρ|=1 is dropped as atanh-singular. Within-city reads and between-city reads are always labeled separately and never averaged across that line.

**2.9 — Honest-null conventions.** Counts verified as values, not keys. Non-reporting months (0/12) excluded, never scored as zero. Point-in-time counts never enter as flows. Between-grain quantities (state shares, county companions, snapshots) stay labeled and never blend into the city-year panel.

### 4.2 Contradicting and disagreeing references — the charitable-engagement ledger

Per the study's standing protocol: each disagreeing work is steel-manned FIRST, then what we accept and carry is named, then the specific dimension where our method differs (the accounted-for variable, the timeline grain, the case class), then what remains genuinely contested versus what resolves. No contrary finding is dismissed.

- **Mahoney JL, Stattin H, Lord H. "Unstructured youth recreation centre participation and antisocial behaviour development." International Journal of Behavioral Development, 2004 (Örebro cohort, N=1,163, 2-year longitudinal, 95 citations).**
  - *Their case, strongest form:* open-drop-in youth centres can RAISE antisocial behaviour over two years for boys and girls after selection controls — peer aggregation beats supervision when programming is unstructured, and centres aggregating many antisocial peers do worst.
  - *What right we accept and carry:* all of their selection logic; the finding directly shaped our structure-beats-open-door law — dollars on STRUCTURED activity (staffed programs) predict differently than dollars on unstructured drop-in capacity, and our care-lane never treats "youth spend" as fungible.
  - *The specific gap our method accounts for:* their exposure is facility participation per youth; ours is budget-line grain per city-year, which prices what the city chooses to fund, not which youth attend; our within-city FE absorbs the time-invariant selection they must model away.
  - *Contested vs resolved:* resolved for our grain — we do not claim facility-level effects; their warning stands as a design constraint we carry, not a contradiction of the city-year claim.
- **Chetty R, Hendren N, Katz LF (MTO adolescent results), American Economic Review, 2016 — the null-to-negative adolescent findings.**
  - *Their case, strongest form:* the flagship mobility experiment finds young-child gains (+31% earnings) and slightly NEGATIVE adolescent effects on the same treatment.
  - *What right we accept and carry:* the full age-structure result — it is the load-bearing justification for our two-lane youth design (under-13 programs separate from 13–18 alternatives) and for never averaging age bands.
  - *The specific gap our method accounts for:* our instruments observe the city's BUDGET choice, not the household's relocation; the adolescent result constrains mobility-style treatments, and our lane adds the budget-side variables (which activity structures the city funds at which ages) the experiment cannot vary.
  - *Contested vs resolved:* their adolescent null-to-negative is accepted as valid in its own domain (relocation timing); what resolves for us is that no municipal-budget instrument we register is a relocation instrument, so the two claims operate at different treatment classes.
- **Raphael S, Stoll MA, and the deinstitutionalization-skeptics class (psychiatric-beds literature, incl. the beds-vs-spend null rows PMID 42750086; Prins; Webster 2014 Missouri competing-control).**
  - *Their case, strongest form:* jail/prison expansion, not psychiatric deinstitutionalization, is the dominant criminal-justice-supply story of the era; the naive beds-collapsed-so-crime-rose narrative overclaims; handgun-policy shifts (e.g., Missouri's permit-repeal) confound within our window.
  - *What right we accept and carry:* the rejection of the naive beds story is OUR position too — the beds-vs-spend lane in our own data finds the beds-murder association −0.10 with collinearity 0.006 between beds and spend (they are separable inputs, and beds does not carry the signal spend does).
  - *The specific gap our method accounts for:* their designs price institutional SUPPLY (beds, incarceration rates); ours prices the municipal BUDGET choice set (care dollars vs cage dollars vs bookings) with within-city fixed effects and the bookings lane measured separately — the cage channel (+0.419 FE-2 beta) is reported as its own input, not absorbed into a beds aggregate.
  - *Contested vs resolved:* the incarceration-crime association resolves for our grain (cage dollars enter positive as a control, direction-class), while the macro deinstitutionalization question remains theirs — genuinely outside what city-budget panels can price.
- **The municipal-efficiency literature as counter-read on the residual instrument (DEA single-score class; Tobit second-stage determinants class; Knight et al. rainy-day-fund determinants, Public Budgeting & Finance; Hanson enterprise-zone evaluations).**
  - *Their case, strongest form:* efficiency is a single frontier score per unit (DEA) or a crisis-probability function of fiscal structure (rainy-day, pension, debt determinants); outcome-per-dollar doses for municipal programs are largely absent from print because they are hard to identify.
  - *What right we accept and carry:* their identifications — the DEA class is our calibration reference, the determinants class is our operational-tier input list, and their honest finding that per-line municipal outcome doses are missing is what our two-tier seam fills; where their enterprise-zone evaluations find null-to-small effects (Hanson), our economy lane's coin-level instrument (0.50) sits inside exactly that bracket, which is why it stays gate-shut.
  - *The specific gap our method accounts for:* the multiplication identity (§2.7) — they measure one tier; we keep overhead-rate and outcome-yield as distinct quantities and compose them, with a 2×2 scorecard distinguishing wrong-programs from leaky-pipes failure classes.
  - *Contested vs resolved:* either tier alone is their domain (respectfully carried); the JOINT decomposition and the 2×2 assembly are ours — zero hits in a 428-record live-resolved corpus is the countable state of that seam, and a non-keyless sweep is the named open test.
- **The recreation-participation null band (sports-participation meta-analysis, 132,366 adolescents / 431 effect sizes ≈ zero aggregate; after-school meta 17 studies small-positive; midnight-basketball early-adopter property-crime falls, confounded).**
  - *Their case, strongest form:* aggregate youth-sport and after-school programming shows ≈ zero average crime effect; the celebrated midnight-basketball declines are confounded at adoption.
  - *What right we accept and carry:* the aggregate null — it is why our lane refuses to treat "parks & recreation dollars" as one lever and instead decomposes the care components (libraries −0.59, welfare-other −0.43, health −0.35, parks +0.19 in the component beta table), where the composition, not the aggregate, carries the fit (joint R² 0.086 vs summed 0.001).
  - *The specific gap our method accounts for:* the aggregate null is consistent with offsetting components; our component decomposition tests exactly that hypothesis and finds the offsets real — the libraries-vs-parks split is the accounted-for variable their aggregate design cannot see.
  - *Contested vs resolved:* their null stands for aggregate programming; our composition claim is scoped to the component-grain panel and is the one dimension they could not resolve by design.
- **Jacob & Ludwig 2014 (vouchers raised adult crime) as partial counter to the housing strand.**
  - *Their case, strongest form:* relaxing a housing constraint with vouchers can RAISE adult offending (less-constrained neighborhood choice), complicating any "better housing → less crime" reading.
  - *What right we accept and carry:* the mechanism — household constraint relief changes crime through behavior channels our rent-burden beta does not price.
  - *The specific gap our method accounts for:* our housing input is shelter STRAIN (cost burden, home value) at city-year grain within-city, not a household-level voucher experiment; the class-flip of CLAIM-1's food strand and the within-FE frame are where the two grains genuinely differ.
  - *Contested vs resolved:* their experimental adult-crime result stands in its domain; our panel claim on shelter strain is correlational by design and carries that label; the two are compatible — strain and constraint-relief are different treatments.
