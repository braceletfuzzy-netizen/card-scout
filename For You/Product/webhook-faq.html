How to Set Up Your Discord Alerts

Card Scout delivers real-time market alerts directly to your personal Discord server using a secure "Webhook." It only takes a minute to set up.

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Open Discord and go to your personal Server.
  2. Click the server name at the top left to open the dropdown menu.
  3. Click Server Settings.
  4. In the left sidebar, click Integrations.
  5. Click Webhooks.
  6. Click the New Webhook button.
  7. Give your bot a name (like "Card Scout") and choose which channel you want the alerts to drop into.
  8. Click the Copy Webhook URL button.
  9. Click Save Changes.

Where to Paste It

Go back to the Card Scout My Monitor page. Find the Webhook field at the top, paste the URL you just copied, and click Update.

Privacy & Permissions (The "One-Way" Rule):
A webhook is strictly a one-way street. It only gives Card Scout permission to send messages into that specific channel. We cannot read your messages, see who is in your server, or access your Discord account.

Pro Tip: You can easily mute the channel in Discord if you want the alerts to pile up silently for you to review later without your phone buzzing.
