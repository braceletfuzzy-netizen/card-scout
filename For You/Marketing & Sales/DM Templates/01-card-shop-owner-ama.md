# DM: Card Shop Owner (AMA)

**Target**: Card shop owner who did an AMA on Reddit
**Goal**: Get them to try Card Scout free for a month
**Best version**: Option 1 (shortest, most authentic)

---

## Option 1 ⭐ RECOMMENDED (3 sentences)

> "Hey, saw your AMA about the card shop business. That line about 90% of profit coming from singles really hit home — I built a Discord bot that watches eBay for underpriced cards and alerts you when deals pop up. Tracks 50+ cards, finds inventory while you sleep. Want to try it free for a month? No strings."

**Why this works:**
- Shows you read his AMA (not mass spam)
- References his specific insight (90% singles)
- Clear value (50+ cards, inventory finding)
- One clear ask (free month)
- No pressure ("no strings")

> "Hey, caught your AMA yesterday. Curious — when you're looking for singles to stock, how do you usually find underpriced cards? Manual eBay searching, or do you have a system?

## 📋 Before Sending Checklist

- [ ] Did you spell the username right?
- [ ] Does it reference the AMA specifically?
- [ ] Is it under 4 sentences?
- [ ] Did you avoid salesy language?
- [ ] Is the ask crystal clear (free month)?
- [ ] Would YOU reply to this?

**If all yes → send it today while the AMA is fresh.**

---

## 🔄 Follow-Up Strategy

**If he replies (even "sure" or "what is it?"):**
- Send 2-3 sentences explaining
- Ask for his Discord webhook URL
- Set him up in 5 minutes
- "You'll get your first alerts Mon/Wed/Fri at 9am EST"

**If he doesn't reply (after 3-5 days):**
- Don't send another DM (looks desperate)
- Try commenting on his next post
- Or wait for his next AMA (3 months)

**If he says "not interested":**
- "No worries! Thanks for the AMA, it was really helpful."
- Leave on good terms
- He might refer you later
