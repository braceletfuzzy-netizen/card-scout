## Version 7: Technical Pitch (30 seconds)

**Best for:** Technical audiences, developers, engineers

> "Card Scout is a Discord alert service built on top of an Apify scraping actor.
>
> The actor scrapes eBay for a given search query, extracts price data, calculates market statistics (median, Q1, Q3, average), and returns structured data.
>
> The Discord bot runs the actor daily for each customer's watchlist, filters for listings below their alert threshold (Q1, Q3, or median), and posts formatted embeds to their Discord webhook.
>
> It costs $0.04 per search and customers pay $50/month for up to 30 cards. That's 95%+ margin with minimal infrastructure."

**Why it works:** Technical credibility, shows you understand the stack

## 💡 The Formula (For Future Pitches)

Every good pitch needs:
1. **WHAT** you do
2. **WHO** it's for
3. **PROBLEM** you solve
4. **SOLUTION** you offer
5. **PROOF** it works (specific numbers)
6. **PRICING** (optional but powerful)

Stories beat facts. Specifics beat generalities. Proof beats claims.
